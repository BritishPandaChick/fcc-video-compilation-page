KPOP Demon Hunters Golden Performances

Netflix announced in 2025 they were intending to put "Golden" from KPOP Demon Hunters for the Academy Awards for Best Song. Here are the musical performances they Huntrx girls made throughout the award show season.

The Tonight Show

The first official live performance of "Golden" was on The Tonight Show. Prior to the performance, Jimmy Fallon announced the KPOP Demon Hunters soundtrack went plantinum.

Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade

The Huntrx girls announced they were performing at the annual Thanksgiving day parade. The girls performed near the end of the parade before Santa Claus arrived. This performance was held outside of Macy's Deparatment store and featured Derpy and Sussie balloons next to the stage.

Academy Awards 2026

Huntrx were announced as one of the two performances for the Best Song category. This performance featured the Hunter's mantra prior to the Huntrx girls singing Golden.
